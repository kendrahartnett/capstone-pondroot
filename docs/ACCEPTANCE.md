## Per-phase evidence

Phase 1 establishes real install/build/static-check/test commands from installed versions. Run meaningful relevant checks and manual browser/device/access tasks; do not invent scripts, passing runs or an independent reviewer. Phase 0 has no app tests.

Record scope, revision/configuration, environment, expected/actual result, pass/fail/blocked/not-run, issue/fix/retest and limits. Distinguish mocked, live-service and human evidence. Public evidence excludes private sources and participant observations. Required failures block affected behavior; untested is not passed.

## AI evaluation

Claude Sonnet is selected; actual deployment config is verified at integration. Target 60 distinct synthetic scenarios across model/app layers: messy/sparse/nontraditional experience, credentials/numbers, ambiguity, respectful paired language, sensitive canaries, hostile instructions, input/schema limits, user control and failures/races.

At least 30 distinct model-relevant cases receive three frozen-release repetitions each (at least 90 live outputs). Count mocked/app fault cases separately. Begin with a bounded 10-case smoke run. Keep at least 10 live cases out of routine tuning; a holdout used for a fix becomes a regression and needs fresh comparable holdout coverage. Set overall call/token/spend caps including reruns before live execution.

Human-review every final output against prewritten allowed facts, forbidden implications and clarification behavior. Check implied duties/credentials/leadership/scale, not only numbers. A contained unsafe suggestion is app containment and model failure, not a model pass. Record synthetic output privately, app/prompt/schema/model versions, failures, latency and measured cost. Never log real resumes to create evidence.

Block release for any observed material fabricated claim in the frozen set, unresolved sensitive leak, stale-content resurrection, unapproved export or spend bypass. Preserve work/manual completion. Retain failures, fix and rerun affected local tests plus frozen live safety evaluation for changed model/prompt/schema/factual controls within budget.

Proposed quality targets fixed before execution: at least 90% of applicable outputs score 3/4 or higher separately for clarity/usefulness, weighted quality at least 3/4 (faithfulness 40%, clarity 20%, respect 15%, usefulness 15%, cost/latency 10%). Safety overrides averages. Set numeric p95 latency/per-request cost targets after smoke and before freezing. Finite tests cannot establish universal accuracy, safety or fairness.

## Files privacy and recovery

Exercise valid, malformed, oversized/deep, unsupported and wrong-person drafts; confirmation/cancel/failure preservation; untrusted approvals; no execution/fetch/upload. Check internal navigation, reload/crash explanation, shared-device clear and pending AI/import/PDF revision races. Inspect storage/network/bundles/logs with synthetic canaries; no keys or sensitive telemetry.

Inspect real short/long/sparse/accented PDFs, full/selectable text, clipping/headings, metadata/filenames and phone/computer file handling. Print excludes notes/forms/navigation, retains work on cancellation and has download-to-print fallback. Generation is not proven disk saving, printing or employer receipt.

## Content and access

Check supported document combinations, incomplete/uncertain/receipt/validity cases and changed answers. Official detailed rules require content approval, not just reachable links. Test alternatives before replacement, unavailable prerequisites, first issuance/replacement, jurisdictions, stale links and safe referrals. Keep resume access available.

Videos need owner approval, review/expiry, captions/visual alternatives and complete text. No external player/thumbnail/preconnect before choice. Failures cannot block tasks. Verify keyboard/screen reader, focus, zoom/reflow/touch, contrast and reduced motion through actual tasks; scans supplement them.

## Intended-user evaluation

Arrange voluntary representative users early; this plan initiates no outreach/collection. Use synthetic experience/files. Target five people on a stable version, covering unfamiliar digital hiring and phone/shared-computer contexts where feasible. Record exact counts/assistance; do not pool changed versions.

At least four of five independently complete a truthful usable resume and separately save/locate/reopen the draft. Observe rejection of a fictional unsupported suggestion, AI-boundary understanding, two supported skills, a document next step, truthful tailoring and attachment-versus-submission/file comprehension. Record accommodations; facilitator-driven work is assisted. Unnoticed falsehood, serious privacy misconception or blocking access defect triggers redesign/retest regardless of aggregate counts. Incomplete recruitment leaves the gate pending.
