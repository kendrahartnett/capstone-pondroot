# Pondroot detailed screen flow
November 20, 2026 — Prepare, Apply, Connect through Rooted Network

November 26 direction: keep the guest preparation flow. Rooted Network browse must show an honest zero-employer state until real verified opportunities exist. Add a public Partner With Pondroot route for employer and workforce inquiries with a secure, minimal-data process. Candidate profile and introduction screens remain designed future states; no account, matching, or sharing is triggered by browsing. See PHASES.md for the revised RN3–RN7 checkpoints.

## Information architecture
Primary destinations: My next steps, Build my résumé, Rooted Network, Check my documents, Transparent Hiring Information, Sign in/My space. Secondary support links use an accessible menu or expanded details. Preserve work across internal navigation. Show one dominant next action. Use ordinary browser Back behavior without losing entered data.

## S01 Welcome and next steps
Show original logo, bridge value, community/roots artwork away from text. Start with my strengths leads to S02; Go straight to my résumé to S05. Rooted Network leads to S11 without forcing discovery. Independent documents S14 and information S13 remain accessible. Returning session shows Continue my résumé. Avoid registration wall and invented partner counts. Risk: too many choices; keep supporting actions visually secondary.

## S02 Conversation
One plain question: Tell us about something you have done. Explain that work, home, school and helping others can count. Speak and Type have equal access; skip leads to editor. Speak explains processing before microphone permission; active recording shows status and Stop. Denial, unsupported browser, silence or network error returns to Type with existing text. No auto recording or default audio storage. Risk: uncertainty about what counts; provide an optional concrete example without prefilled claims.

## S03 Transcript and facts
Show editable transcript or typed answer. Confirm these facts creates current source records; Edit/Delete/Back remain available. Unconfirmed transcript is not evidence. Ask concrete follow ups only for missing relevant facts; unknown dates are allowed. No incarceration or gap explanation requirement. Risk: misheard facts; confirmation must be deliberate.

## S04 AI consent and suggestion review
From the conversation, show the exact confirmed words selected for AI. **Continue with these words** is the explicit send; opening the panel sends nothing. Return a short résumé draft with an introduction, skills, and task wording only when supported. Show each part beside its source and an unchecked **Use this in my résumé** box. Let the user edit wording in place, then choose **Use checked parts** once. Unchecked parts stay out. Editing a source removes dependent old wording. Provider limit/error offers manual writing. Never infer employer, date, title, credential, or achievement.

## S05 Résumé workspace
Labeled editable contact, introduction, skills, experience, education and optional training/additional sections. Add/remove/exclude entries with clear recovery. No guessed filler. Desktop live preview; mobile Edit/Preview retains position. Help me write routes to selected fact consent. Review résumé leads S06. Save editable draft is always deliberate. Risk: form overload; progressive section disclosure and short examples.

## S06 Review
Show exact employer-facing content and a single **Edit résumé** link. Unapproved content is omitted and clearly flagged. The visible résumé is the final review surface. The two main actions below it are **Download PDF** and **Print résumé**. Never force unsupported filler or shrink unreadable content to one page.

## S07 File actions
**Download PDF** prepares the current version and opens S08 when ready; there is no separate Prepare button. **Print résumé** opens the native print view without email. Save editable draft remains an optional secondary control in the editor. File-location help and next steps are expandable details. Failed/cancelled actions preserve work; late PDF work cannot restore a cleared session.

## S08 Download email
Ask valid email for Download PDF and explain impact follow up purpose. Separate unchecked permission: May Pondroot email you once to ask whether this helped you move toward work? No/unchecked still downloads and address is discarded; Yes authorizes one message with deletion afterward or within 90 days. Show privacy/deletion contact. Download does not create account, matching permission or automatic emailed résumé. Cancel returns S07. Some print dialogs offer Save as PDF; describe accurately.

## S09 Application next step
Offer one concrete application action, Transparent Hiring Information/ATS, reviewed support card and Explore Rooted Network. Explain upload versus submitting. Career guidance uses reviewed facts and general preparation; no submission, job promise or legal verdict. Save online is optional S10.

## S10 Account and my space
Explain secure saving, then managed sign up/sign in and recovery. Show guest content before explicit Save online. On interruption keep local work; on conflicting remote revision ask which version to retain. My space lists saved résumés, next step, profile sharing state, Export/Delete/Sign out. Account creation leaves matching off. Sign out clears visible private session; Clear guest session and Delete cloud records have distinct explanations.

## S11 Rooted Network
Explain verified employer connections in plain words. Browse opportunities without account; display title/location, requirements, source/review date, expiry and application route. Why this may fit uses confirmed skills/preferences only. Empty inventory offers preparation/resources without fictitious employers. View partner explains verification scope; verification is not a guarantee of conduct or hire.

## S12 Shared profile and introduction
Want partners to consider your profile? Yes/No, default No. No returns to browsing and preserves résumé. Yes routes through account if needed, then exact selected profile preview and scope. Explain who sees it, contact route, duration and revoke control. Confirming matching does not approve every introduction. For a selected opportunity show current profile, reason and fields to send; Approve introduction/Decline. Dispatch checks current consent/profile/job/partner. Show pending/sent/withdrawn/error honestly; retry is idempotent. Pause matching blocks new access; explain received information cannot be recalled.

## S13 Transparent Hiring Information
Short written lessons: ATS, application steps, required/preferred qualifications, truthful tailoring, finding/uploading files, interview practice and resources. Optional reviewed video loads only after choice. No ATS pass/score guarantee, course gate or sensitive answer capture. Return to résumé/Rooted Network preserves context.

## S14 Documents and support
Reviewed deterministic guidance and official obtaining help; general A OR B+C alternatives, unsure routes to official help. No scans, numbers or legal eligibility verdict. Support card describes external destination/prototype/account needs, opens safely without personal data handoff, and preserves Pondroot work. Unreviewed resources remain unpublished.

## S15 Recovery and end session
Open draft validates locally, summarizes and confirms before replacement. Failure/cancel keeps work; imported AI text needs renewed review and never imports sharing consent. Clear offers save/keep working/clear; invalidate late operations before resetting. Explain local downloaded files and provider processing cannot be erased by Clear. Cloud deletion separately confirms affected records and retention.

## Cross screen acceptance
Semantic headings/labels, visible focus, logical keyboard order, announced errors/status, touch targets, readable contrast, mobile reflow and reduced motion. Focus returns to the triggering control after dialogs; no keyboard traps. Never rely on color, speech, animation or imagery alone. Screens link to RN-A01–RN-A14 in ACCEPTANCE. Evaluate comprehension, emotional burden and access with intended users; screenshots alone do not establish accessibility.
