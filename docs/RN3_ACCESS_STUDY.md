# RN3 human access and comprehension check

## Ready-to-schedule session packet

Run one 30-minute screen-reader session and five separate 25-minute intended-user sessions on the same reviewed build. The facilitator provides a device or accessible link, one synthetic experience card, and a synthetic example of a false AI suggestion. Start with: “We are testing Pondroot, not you. You may stop at any time. Please use the example story rather than your own personal information. We will note task outcomes and problems, not record your voice or save your story.” Ask permission to take de-identified task notes; a decline means no study record. No names, emails, incarceration history, personal résumés, screen recordings, or raw audio belong in the notes.

For each session, record date, build commit, browser/device, optional assistive technology, consent to notes, task-by-task independent/assisted/blocked result, exact confusion or incorrect factual claim, and severity. Keep participant codes P1–P5 only in a private local record; publish aggregate counts and issue summaries. The owner supplies participants and scheduling; no outreach is sent by Pondroot without a separately approved recipient and message.

Facilitator sequence: (1) show the synthetic story card; (2) observe entry by typing or optional voice and transcript correction; (3) ask the participant which facts AI will receive and what happens if they cancel; (4) present supported and unsupported suggestions and observe source review, rejection, edit and confirmation; (5) finish a truthful résumé; (6) save, find and reopen an editable draft; (7) explain Print and Download; (8) ask what they would do if AI or voice were unavailable. Do not coach during the first attempt. Afterward ask what wording or control was confusing. The screen-reader session adds landmark, label, focus, status, reading-order, zoom/reflow and language-announcement checks.

Use this exact synthetic card for all five intended-user sessions: “My name is Sample Person. I cleaned rooms and changed linens. I earned my GED. I helped my grandmother cook meals at home. I do not remember employer names or dates.” The clearly false test suggestion is “Certified housekeeping supervisor with five years of management experience.” Tell participants that any AI suggestion shown during a local facilitated session is a synthetic test response, not a live model result. Do not invite them to enter real histories while the public AI gate is closed.

Local interactive setup: start Pondroot with `npm run dev -- --port 3002`, then in a second terminal run `node scripts/rn3-study-preview.cjs`. This opens a visible browser labeled **TEST SESSION** and intercepts only DiscoverSkills calls with synthetic responses; no Anthropic call or production database reservation occurs. Ask the participant to enter and select the cleaning example first, then review the supported wording and reject the deliberately false supervisor claim. Close the study browser to end interception. This study preview deliberately bypasses the server's normal suggestion filter to test whether a person notices and rejects a false claim; it is never a public release configuration or a model-safety pass.

For the screen-reader session, use the same card and ask the tester to narrate where focus moves after selecting an example, opening draft suggestions, rejecting the false suggestion, returning to the résumé, and changing language. If no accessible test environment can display synthetic suggestions interactively, record those tasks as blocked rather than claiming they passed from a static script. The owner can run the local app at `http://127.0.0.1:3002`; remote participants cannot open that localhost address on their own device, so arrange an in-person session or a reviewed preview deployment.

Use one stable reviewed build and synthetic experience cards. Do not ask participants to enter their own work history, justice history, contact details, or credentials. Record the build commit, device/browser/assistive technology, task outcome, assistance, confusion, and blocking defects without storing personal stories or recordings.

## Screen-reader session

Have a person familiar with a screen reader complete the core RN3 tasks using a real screen reader (for example NVDA on Windows or VoiceOver on a phone), without mouse help. Check heading and landmark navigation, language announcement after English/Español switching, labels and instructions, checkbox state, source-quote association, individual accept/edit/reject, focus after errors and suggestion changes, live status messages, transcript correction, and manual fallback when AI or microphone is unavailable. Repeat core navigation at 320 CSS pixels and 200% text size; check visible focus, reading order, reflow, touch targets and reduced motion. An axe or Playwright pass is supplementary evidence, not this session.

## Five-person intended-user check

Recruit five voluntary representative users for the stable version, including unfamiliar digital hiring and phone/shared-computer contexts where feasible. Give each the same synthetic experience card, plus a deliberately unsupported suggestion to reject. Ask them to describe an experience, choose exact facts for AI, explain what will be sent, review the source quote, reject the false claim, confirm or edit a supported suggestion, finish a truthful résumé, and separately save, locate and reopen an editable draft. Include the voice path when the device permits, and ask what they would do if voice or AI were unavailable. Observe without coaching first; record any help separately.

The existing acceptance target is at least four of five independently completing a truthful usable résumé and separately saving, locating and reopening a draft on the stable version. Record exact denominators and confusion, abandonment, assistance, source understanding, privacy understanding and next-step comprehension. A severe falsehood, privacy misconception or blocking accessibility issue requires a fix and retest regardless of the count. Do not pool results from changed builds or claim recruitment, independent review, or participant outcomes before they occur.

## Owner coordination needed

The owner identifies a suitable screen-reader tester and five voluntary participants, approves the synthetic cards and observation/consent language, and schedules sessions. No outreach or personal-data collection is authorized by this plan alone.
